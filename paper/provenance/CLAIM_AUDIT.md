## Material corrections and excluded claims

- Removed the theory placeholder and any prose presenting prospective theoretical or comparative work as completed.
- Distinguished the retained Gaussian placement winner from the highest-scoring Gaussian variant anywhere in the historical sweep.
- Kept both the frozen-BN Hessian and pointwise-recalibrated finite sensitivity result. Their different Gaussian ordering concerns different functions, coordinates and perturbation scales, not a numerical contradiction to conceal.
- Replaced "all weight sets have their lowest loss in their trained state" and "R-to-RG barriers are never smaller" with the actual exceptions. The barrier exception is wider than first written: on the training probe the R-to-RG barrier is lower than plain-to-R in seeds 1, 2 and 4, and on the test probe in seed 1 (`handover/FINAL_SOURCE_CHECKS.md` §5).
- Separated training-probe CE, online epoch loss, full-training-set CE, and test CE.
- Removed unbacked gauge distances, displaced-prior numbers and claims of a PAC-Bayes or other generalization theorem.
- Kept TV-budget transformations explicitly at the preview/proposal stage. Historical quadratic reconstruction arms are different experiments; no TV training results are invented.
- CBS and SDPoint are cited as antecedents, with no direct empirical comparison or claim of superiority. The handoff says those comparisons had never started.
