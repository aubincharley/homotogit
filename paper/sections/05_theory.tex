\section{A statistical interpretation}
\label{sec:theory}
The observation we would like to explain is a higher training cross-entropy together with a lower test cross-entropy at the end of the same training budget. At epoch 30, on a fixed probe of 500 training images, the cross-entropy is $\uProbeRes$, $\uProbeGauss$ and $\uProbeComb$ for the resolution, Gaussian and combined procedures, against $\uProbePlain$ for the baseline, while the test cross-entropy is $\uTestCERes$, $\uTestCEGauss$ and $\uTestCEComb$ against $\uTestCEPlain$ (means over three seeds, Table~\ref{tab:final-losses}).

% A tractable first setting is a regression model with components that become available at different times. The objective would be to identify conditions under which delayed fitting increases empirical error while decreasing expected prediction error. Early-stopping analyses provide a useful starting point \citep{ali2019early}. Such a result would illustrate a mechanism under stated signal and noise assumptions; it would not by itself prove the explanation for nonlinear max-pooling, BatchNorm or classification cross-entropy. Likewise, a smaller upper bound on risk is not sufficient to order two actual risks.
