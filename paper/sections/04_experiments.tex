\section{Experimental study}
\label{sec:experiments}
\subsection{Reference setting and evaluation}
The exploratory setting uses CIFAR-10, a ResNet-20 with BatchNorm \citep{he2016resnet}, and 30 epochs of SGD without data augmentation. We use all 50,000 training images and evaluate on the 10,000 test images. The selected methods were run in one batch, with the same pinned initial weights and data order within each seed, and evaluated after every epoch. We compare them at the final epoch, on the original inference path, without best-epoch selection. The reference training details and the exploratory tables are given in Appendices~\ref{app:protocol} and~\ref{app:exploration}.

We report means and sample standard deviations over seeds. Differences between methods are paired only when the runs verified the same pinned assets, identified by content hashes, under the same code and conditions. We do not correct results across batches: plain runs on identical assets finished up to $\plainSpreadMax$ points apart in different batches, for reasons we have not established (Appendix~\ref{app:conventions}). The final exploration reused the test set for method selection, so these numbers are descriptive evidence, not an independent assessment of the selected configurations. Training cross-entropy recorded on a fixed 500-image training probe is also distinguished from cross-entropy over the full training sample, which was not evaluated.
