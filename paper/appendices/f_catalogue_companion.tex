\section{Exploration catalogue (companion record)}
\label{app:catalogue}
The complete record of every configuration trained during the CIFAR-10 exploration is
supplied as a companion file rather than printed here: \texttt{catalogue/exploration\_catalogue.pdf}
for reading, \texttt{catalogue/exploration\_catalogue.csv} for machine use, and
\texttt{catalogue/exploration\_catalogue.md} as plain text. It is rebuilt from the run
records by \texttt{paper/tools/build\_records.py} followed by
\texttt{paper/tools/build\_catalogue.py}, and \texttt{catalogue/CHANGE\_LOG.md} states
which values changed, if any, when they were recomputed.

The companion keeps all \idxConfigurations{} configurations behind the
\idxRuns{} recorded runs of \idxExperiments{} experiments, including the configurations
whose runs produced non-finite losses and the separate launches of the same
configuration, each with its accuracy, seed counts and wall time, grouped by experiment
with that experiment's data, budget, evaluation split and timing scope. Nothing was
removed in moving it out of the paper; the decisive comparisons, the controls and the
adverse results that bear on the claims of this paper stay in
Appendix~\ref{app:exploration}, including the failed operator, the reduced-resolution
controls that never restore the native grid, the pilots whose sign disagrees with the
reference protocol, and the statement that the selection reused CIFAR-10 test results.
