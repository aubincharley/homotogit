\section{Complete supplied exploration catalogue}
\label{app:catalogue}
This catalogue preserves the trained configurations underlying the historical exploration, grouped by their actual experimental conditions. It is not a leaderboard across comparable tasks. Accuracy uses the last recorded endpoint; one valid seed has no estimated SD. A fixed intervention is evaluated on its retained inference path and is labelled as a control. Failed configurations remain visible. Time is mean wall time as reported by that study, whose timing scope and source records are preserved in the accompanying provenance file. Different launches are not additional matched seeds of a single experiment.

For readability, internal configuration identifiers and full source paths are in the source catalogue and provenance files; the table below retains the configuration description, final accuracy, seed count and time. The later transfer and diagnostic grids are reported in Appendices~\ref{app:transfer}--\ref{app:input} and are not added to this historical ranking.
